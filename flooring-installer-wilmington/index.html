How to prepare and what to ask

Before the consultation, make a list of the rooms you want to update and note any questions about the existing floors. You don’t need to know the technical answers. Sharing what you’ve noticed helps start a useful conversation.

A simple checklist can keep the discussion focused:

  • Identify the rooms and areas included in the project
  • Gather notes about existing flooring or visible subfloor concerns
  • Consider the colors and finishes you want the new floor to work with
  • Ask about preparation, acclimation, layout, trim and cleanup
  • Mention household routines or rental needs that affect planning

A mistake to avoid is choosing a product before considering how it will be installed. Another is assuming the preparation or trim work is understood without asking. If you’re unsure about the subfloor, room transitions or installation requirements, bring those questions to a flooring professional before work starts.
