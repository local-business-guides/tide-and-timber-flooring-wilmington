Tide & Timber Flooring Shares Guide to Flooring in Wilmington

WILMINGTON, North Carolina (November 8, 2026) Tide & Timber Flooring has published a guide about flooring in Wilmington, along with a YouTube video, a podcast episode and an audio version on SoundCloud. The materials introduce local readers to the store’s shop-at-home service and flooring options for coastal homes and other properties in the area.

The guide covers flooring choices available through Tide & Timber, including luxury vinyl plank, engineered hardwood, solid hardwood, carpet and laminate. The store’s flooring experts bring full-size samples to customers’ homes, so people can compare colors and textures in their own rooms and alongside existing finishes. Seeing samples at home can help when considering how a material will look under the lighting and with the details already in a space.

The guide may be useful to homeowners planning a renovation, busy families comparing materials, and vacation rental owners or investors working on a property. It also introduces Tide & Timber’s PerfectFit installation system, which includes subfloor preparation, acclimation, layout, trim and cleanup. Those steps give readers an overview of what the store’s installation process covers as they consider flooring and plan a project.

Readers can access the video on YouTube and the audio version on SoundCloud. The podcast episode is also available, though no platform was specified. Tide & Timber serves Wilmington and nearby communities, including Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, Kure Beach, Hampstead, Surf City and Leland. Customers can book a free consultation with the store.

About Tide & Timber Flooring

Tide & Timber Flooring is a design-first, shop-at-home flooring store serving the Cape Fear Coast. Its flooring experts bring full-size samples to customers, and its service area includes the Greater Wilmington area and nearby coastal communities.
