A practical checklist before choosing

A little preparation can help you get more from your visit. Before we arrive, think about these points:

  • Which rooms are included, and do they connect to one another?
  • What do you like or dislike about the current flooring?
  • Which colors, cabinets or furnishings should the new floor work with?
  • Is the property a primary home, a busy family space or a vacation rental?
  • Do you have questions about the subfloor, trim or the installation sequence?

One mistake to avoid is selecting a floor before considering how it relates to adjacent rooms.
